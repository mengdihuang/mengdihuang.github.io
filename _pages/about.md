I'm a behavioral researcher with a Ph.D. in Social Psychology from Northwestern University and a M.S. in Applied Statistics. I specialize in designing rigorous studies, analyzing behavioral and survey data at scale, and translating findings into insights that inform product, marketing, and people decisions.
<br>
<br>
My work seeks to understand human motivation, decision-making, and behavior through a range of methodologies. Specifically, I use experiments, surveys, and advanced statistical modeling (regression, multilevel modeling, SEM, causal inference) to analyze behavioral and observational datasets ranging from hundreds to millions of records. I've led projects applying NLP to study social network dynamics across 2M+ user profiles, designed and validated survey instruments across independent samples, and run experiments and field studies to optimize engagement and experience metrics. I enjoy turning ambiguous behavioral questions into measurable, actionable insights.
<br>
<br>
In my free time, I enjoy thrifting, spending time with my dogs, my friends (and their dogs), taking photos for my friends (and their dogs), and exploring new restaurants in Chicago.
